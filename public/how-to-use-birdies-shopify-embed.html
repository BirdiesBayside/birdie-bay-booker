Signing Up & Making a Booking

Getting started with Birdies is easy! Create your account on our Birdies Hub to manage bookings, track your rounds, and access member perks.

Creating Your Account

  1. 1 Visit the Birdies Hub at hub.birdiesbayside.com.au or download the Birdies app
  2. 2 Click "Sign Up" and enter your details (name, email, phone number)
  3. 3 Verify your email address to activate your account
  4. 4 Add a payment method to your account for seamless booking

Making a Booking

  1. 1 Log in to the Birdies Hub and tap "Book Now"
  2. 2 Select your preferred date and time from the availability calendar
  3. 3 Choose your session duration (1-4 hours)
  4. 4 Select the number of players in your group
  5. 5 Confirm and pay using your saved card or account balance

Tip: Members get discounted hourly rates! Consider joining a membership tier for the best value.

Accessing the Centre

Birdies operates without staff during off-peak hours. Here's everything you need to know about getting in for your session.

Door Access Code

After completing your booking, you'll receive an access code via email and SMS. This code is unique to your booking and will work during your booked time slot.

  1. 1 Arrive at the Birdies facility a few minutes before your booking time
  2. 2 Enter your 4-digit access code on the door keypad
  3. 3 The door will unlock and you can enter the facility

Important: Your access code only works during your booked time slot. Make sure to arrive on time!

Boom Gate Access

If you are booking after dark (or before light) you will need to open the business park boom gate. We will include a link to get the boom gate app in your booking email.

Using the Simulators

Our bays are designed for a seamless, automated experience. When your session starts, everything powers on automatically!

Bay Automation

Each bay is equipped with smart automation that handles the setup for you:

  1. 1 Automatic Power On: 3 minutes before your session, the bay will turn on the bay power first — please warm up
  2. 2 Software Launch: GSPRO will begin launching approx 1 minute before your booking time. Please wait until the wait window closes, then you can begin your session

You will receive warnings when your session is about to finish, giving you enough time to book extra time if required. Your bay will close the apps and shut down when your session finishes. If someone has booked after you, the bay will remain on and they can take over your session.

Playing a Birdies League Round

Birdies League uses the SimGolf Tour (SGT) platform to track handicaps, run tournaments, and manage leaderboards. Here's how to participate in league play.

Getting Started with Birdies League

  1. 1 Sign up to the Birdies League in the Hub
  2. 2 Your account will automatically be detected when booking a bay, so the SGT icon will show on the GSPRO menu
  3. 3 Click the SGT icon and follow instructions to add yourself as a Player in GSPRO and access tournaments

Viewing Your League Stats

After playing league rounds, you can view your stats and standings directly in the Birdies Hub:

  • Leaderboard: See where you rank against other players
  • Your Rounds: Review all your previous league rounds and scorecards
  • Handicap: Track your handicap progression over time
